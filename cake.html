Cakes

Cake is a flour confection made from flour, sugar and other ingredients and is usuallly baked. In their oldest forms, cakes were modifications of bread, but cake now cover a wide range of preparation thar can be simple or elaborate and which share feature with dessert such as pastries, meringues, custard, and pies.

Cake is often served as a celebratory dish on ceremonial occasions such as weddings, anniversaries, and birthdays. There are countless cake recipes, some are bread-like, some are rich and elaborate, and many are centuries old. Cake making is no longer a complicated procedure, while at one time considerable labor went into cake making (particularly the whiskey of egg foams), baking equipment and directions have been simplified so that even the most amateur of cooks may bake a cake.
