Soup

Soup is one of the world's oldest and most universal foods, said janet clarkson, author of the book "soup: A Global History" "Every culture has some kind of soup," she said "it's got very ancient roots," Early people simmered it in everthing from turtle shell to lenght of bamboo, she write in the book, turning out metal spoon pots starting in the Bronze Age.

Boiling food made it possible to subsist on stable grains,with herbs and other ingredients added for nourishment or medicinal purposes. Each time you deliver a pot of hearty soup (with perhaps a side of bread) to a friend with the sniffles, clarkson said you're infact carrying an age-old tradition. "seperating food and medicine - that not how the ancient people thought of it", she said "I think in every country of the world, historically, same soups were seen as restorative."
